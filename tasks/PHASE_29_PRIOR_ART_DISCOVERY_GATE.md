# Phase 29 - Prior-Art Discovery Gate

## Purpose

Turn the Gate 0 research protocol into a reusable harness module.

## Positioning

Earlier phases use focused research checkpoints. Phase 29 makes discovery
reusable and operator-aware.

## Required outputs

- Discovery Packet;
- source inventory;
- maturity assessment;
- adopt/adapt/reject decisions;
- contradiction findings;
- gaps and unknowns;
- freshness/risk notes;
- next verification steps.

## Non-goals

- Not a generic research archive.
- Not a reason to block all implementation indefinitely.
- Not a replacement for repo/task source-of-truth.
